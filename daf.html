My current role at DAF sits at the intersection of data operations and organizational performance. This is what that looks like in practice.

Why data work belongs in an OPWL portfolio
Organizational performance and data systems share the same core problem: information that should flow clearly gets stuck, distorted, or lost. Whether I'm designing an onboarding workflow or documenting an airflow pipeline, the instinct is identical — understand the system, find the friction, remove it. This section shows that instinct applied to a data environment.
